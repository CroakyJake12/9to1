## Four owner decisions — confirmed

### Q1. Sculpt is the Blender fork

Confirmed by the owner on 22 November 2026. Sculpt retains the broad compatible Blender-derived application scope, including modelling/sculpting, materials, animation, procedural work, simulation, rendering/compositing and supported extensions. Its Games integration consumes canonical assets; it does not limit Sculpt to a Games-only editor. The spec adds APP-SCULPT-001 and ED-SCULPT-001–004. The new workspace is an editable design preview, not an embedded Blender engine or a claim that donor parity is implemented.

### Q2. Mail and Connect are the same app

Confirmed. **Connect** is the single canonical application/package; **Mail** is its email mode and compatible alias. Email, messaging and calls share the app shell while retaining correct protocol-specific data and delivery semantics. Hubs and pins resolve old Mail entries to Connect without duplicating app identity. Existing email routes remain valid.

### Q3. Temporary is a Chat toggle with associated personalisation

Confirmed: **Temporary** is an on/off toggle within normal Chat, not a separate destination or response mode. Its associated **Personalised / Unpersonalised** choice is available in Options before the first message. Personalised may use eligible existing memory, custom instructions and plugins; Unpersonalised excludes those three. Neither personalisation choice permits Temporary-origin content to write memory. The choice locks when the conversation begins, while permissions can still narrow effective access. Remember the eligible account/workspace choice, with policy-constrained Personalised as the first-use state.

Explicit Save converts to a regular locally durable conversation; normal account/workspace settings then apply. Saving does not itself grant a plugin, opt into training, move data to Mesh/Cloud or start work. 9to1 retains its own lifetime, retention and local-save-before-replication contract. The new preview uses only fixed illustrative messages and demonstrates cancelled/failed/successful conversion.

Official interaction references checked 22 November 2026: [Temporary chat](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8914046-temporary-chat-in-chatgpt), [Memory](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8590148-memory-in-chatgpt), and [Projects](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/10169521-projects-in-chatgpt). These inform the interaction pattern; OpenAI-specific retention periods are not imported into 9to1.

### Q4. Train's initial breadth is confirmed

Fine-tuning, Dreaming, evaluation and checkpoint management are the initial scope. General pretraining and arbitrary direct manual weight editing are deferred to a later contract. This does not defer the real training updates, held-out evaluation, promotion gates or rollback already required by Dreaming.

The existing questions about custom material/accent composition, Study scoring thresholds and Industrial versus Metallic remain in the decision register. An unedited or unreviewed screen does not resolve a pending decision.
