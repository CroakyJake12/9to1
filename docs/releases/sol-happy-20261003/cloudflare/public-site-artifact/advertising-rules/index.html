Advertising Rules

The following rules apply to anyone who is currently, or is looking to, advertise on our subreddits. Please note that serious rule violations that occur after an ad has been listed (for example, you add illegal content to your promoted website) may result in your ad getting delisted early without a refund, to protect the CAKE brand.

Advertising Rules

  1. Use common sense when advertising on our subreddits. Your ad, and the content you are linking to, must follow all of the rules of the subreddit you are advertising on and all of CAKE’s general rules.
  2. Your advert must accurately reflect the site you are linking to, ie no clickbait. For example, you cannot advertise a “Fortnite Stat Tracker” where the link actually takes you to a Minecraft server list. If the contents of your site changes drastically within the duration of the advert, please let the mod team know as you may need to update your ad.
  3. Your link must not be a malicious link. This includes (but is not limited to): IP grabbers, Malware, Tracking Software, Deceptive Redirects, Phishing, Scams, Cryptominers, Botnets, Adware, and NSFW.
  4. Your linked site must not intentionally redirect our users onto any site or page which breaks our rules, or which encourages or enables you to break the TOS of another service (eg a YouTube video downloader).
  5. Your linked site and advert must not contain anything that may harm CAKE’s public image, such as controversial political opinions.
  6. Do not use your advert to spread misinformation, disinformation, or unverified rumours. Avoid “rabbit hole” topics such as Anti-Vax. Do not impersonate an individual or organisation in your ad.
  7. Advertisers must have the rights to all content, branding, and intellectual property that they have used in their ad.
  8. Do not intentionally attempt to trigger any phobias or sensitivities.
  9. Your ad, and the content your ad is linking to, must not contain ANY NSFW imagery, references, or discussion.
  10. The linked site must not contain excessive or rule-breaking ads.
  11. Follow Reddit’s TOS at all times.

We may update these terms at any time, and updated terms will affect ads that are currently running. However, if we update our rules and your ad breaks a new rule, we must give at least 7 days’ notice before taking any action against that ad1.

If your ad breaks any of our rules2 at any point during its runtime, we reserve the right to freeze your ad. This means that we will temporarily stop showing your ad, but once the issue is fixed, the ad will be reinstated. If it’s appropriate, for example a rule was broken by accident, we may add extra time to the ad’s runtime to compensate for the downtime. We will usually contact you first before freezing the ad, unless the issue is urgent.

If we have made reasonable efforts to contact you in order to fix your ad, and you have failed to comply or failed to respond, we reserve to completely delist your ad, ending its runtime prematurely. If you believe that your ad has been delisted unfairly, please follow the appeals process found here.

CAKE uses a third-party tool to create subreddit banners, so we are not responsible for any unexpected issues or outages that may occur. However, if a service outage or issue does occur, we will try to work with the owner of the tool to fix the issue asap and we will extend the duration of your ad to compensate you if it led to a downtime or reduction of service for your ad or linked site.

We are unable to offer refunds for adverts once the money has been transferred.

1 – Exceptions may apply if the rule break is severe.
2 – The rules listed above are not conclusive; there may be unforeseen issues that arise that the rules do not cover. In these cases, the 7-day rule must be followed.
