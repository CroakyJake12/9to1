Advertise with CAKE

🚀 Start driving traffic today with an affordable, high-impact Reddit ad!

CAKE Subreddits allow you to advertise products on certain subreddits using the subreddit’s banner (the first thing most members see when they visit the subreddit). Prices vary depending on the size and type of subreddit, and the duration of the ad.

How does it work?

Most of our subreddits feature a premium banner space – it’s the first thing people see when they open the subreddit. Each image on the banner carousel is a clickable link, one of which can be used to take users directly to your product. You can put a title, image, and description, as well as badges like “important!”*

You can Promote Anything You Want; there’s no need to match the subreddit’s theme! Whether you’re selling merch, launching an app, or promoting a UEFN map, our banner promotion gets you noticed by large, targeted communities.

If the subreddit doesn’t have a banner, we might be able to offer a pinned post promotion instead.

Please note that all ads are subject to our Advertising Rules.

How do I advertise?

To get started, simply reach out to the moderators via Modmail:

  • On desktop, scroll down the sub’s sidebar and click “Message Mods.”
  • On mobile, tap the subreddit’s name to open the About section, then hit “Message Mods.”

Once you’re there, just send us a message with the details of what you’d like to advertise. A moderator will get back to you shortly to discuss pricing, available options, and how we can help get your promotion in front of the right audience.
