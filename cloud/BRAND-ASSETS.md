# Website brand assets

The repository owner supplied the 9to1 and CakeMods logos and requested their use in the rebuilt sites and shared 9to1 Account interface. These are brand assets, not generated substitutes. Their inclusion records that project-specific authorization; it does not grant an additional trademark or third-party reuse licence.

The same source PNGs are retained in `cloud/cake-id-auth/public` and `cloud/public-sites/public`. The embedded brand modules contain those assets for Worker delivery. Preserve the source images when changing the build.
