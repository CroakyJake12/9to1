# Custom colour background materials

A custom fill keeps the colour, transparency and Gaussian backdrop radius of each active colour. `CuiCustomMaterialBrush` validates and copies the portable `CuiCustomFill` and is a background descriptor for the canonical CUI surface adapter. It is not a foreground or icon brush. Ordinary foreground/icon resources must keep their normal fill brushes.

The shared adapter builds one bounded Skia filter graph against the original backdrop. Each distinct radius has its own Gaussian filter input; radius zero retains the original input. A gradient uses the same stop weights for colours and for crossfading the per-stop Gaussian results. Linear, elliptical radial and CSS-angle conic geometry share their authored parameters. This is a weighted blend of the authored Gaussian results, not substitution of the largest radius or successive blurring of already-filtered output. Equal radii share a branch. Two to sixteen validated stops bound the graph.

Pattern foreground and background occupy mutually exclusive regions. Stripes, checkerboard and dots retain their exact cell size, spacing and rotation around the surface centre. Each region selects its own Gaussian result; its own colour transparency then reveals that result. A fully transparent foreground region reveals the original/filtered backdrop, not the other pattern colour. The patterned tint and backdrop masks use the same vector geometry, with no application screenshot or captured source texture.

The material draws before the existing control foreground. It does not rewrite the document, alter hit testing, duplicate controls, reparent content, animate, or schedule a refresh timer. The typed surface is redrawn by the current renderer when its source or layout changes. Its shader and filter graph are retained with the draw operation and released with that operation. The registered Skia capability is required explicitly. High-contrast resource projection must replace the custom descriptor with the canonical solid fallback; it must not leave a stale custom descriptor active.

This explicit custom Gaussian choice is independent of Professional's built-in material. Selecting another built-in does not implicitly enable Professional Mica. Integration must bind background-specific role resources rather than use material markers for text or icon paint. Source and native pixel checks do not establish browser performance, account persistence, custom editor behaviour, packaged native acceptance or whole-app completion.
