# Revision evidence and authority hierarchy

1. **Authoritative specifications:** official OCR H446 and H418 specification PDFs in this package, and Pearson Edexcel 9MA0 official specification (the full Pearson PDF is *not* bundled; use the existing mapped Maths guide plus current official Pearson documents).
2. **Specific marking:** exact official past-paper mark scheme and examiner's report for the specific sitting and question, when available. Never invent commentary or replace a real scheme with generic guidance. Keep mark-by-mark citation to scheme identifiers/pages.
3. **Subject law:** legislation.gov.uk and reported judgments/BAILII/UKSC as appropriate, checking dates and amendments. An exam board's framing of a case may emphasize specific exam criteria; distinguish this from legal doctrine.
4. **Class/college materials:** useful for which topics have been taught and local mock boundaries, but not proof of statutory or doctrinal correctness.
5. **Student personal notes and retrieval answers:** evidence of prior attempts or explanations, not independently authoritative.
6. **Model recollection:** lowest authority; mark anything not verified as unverified.

Attribution: when responding from a local source, cite the file name and a topic/section (page where verified). Do not fabricate official marks, statutory citations, test execution or visual evidence.

Do not import or publish these files to a public repository without deliberate owner choice; school notes and learning state should remain private.
