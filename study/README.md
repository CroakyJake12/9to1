# Local revision workspace

This folder contains offline exports of existing personal Google documents and supplied specifications, a learner progress record and a diagnostic. Keep it locally for Qwen/OpenCode. PDFs and DOCX files are unchanged exports; TXT exports may omit layout, equations, illustrations and Google Doc tabs not represented in plain text. For diagrams and original handwriting, follow the tldraw links in the Maths working record.

**Attention:** the Maths 9MA0 TXT export is an in-depth personal guide, not the original official specification. The H446 and H418 PDFs are original copies of specifications from the connected Drive account. The two CS PoL files are college programme documents. Law checklists and retrieval logs are personal working materials, not mark schemes. They have not been independently revalidated by this installer.

For a source-backed response: locate a section in the relevant file, check the specification and question, and use local tests for maths/programming. In Law, verify any contentious doctrine against primary legal authority and OCR guidance.

`study/state` is gitignored deliberately; progress is private and stays editable. The installer will not replace existing study state.
