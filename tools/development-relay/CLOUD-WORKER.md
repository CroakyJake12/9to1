For an authorised relay shift, verify the supplied base and the current lease before reading or changing the ticket’s assigned files. Preserve the receiver’s original checkpoint, tests, unresolved failures and requested platform checks. Follow the configured heartbeat and stop-write behaviour when the lease expires. Hand the exact resulting source and receipts back to the named coordinator before releasing ownership.
