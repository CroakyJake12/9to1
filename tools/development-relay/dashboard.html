    How progress and target scopes are measured +

    80% development · 20% acceptance

    Weighted estimate = development percentage × 0.8 + independent acceptance percentage × 0.2. Development counts recorded source, interface, build and journey milestones. A passing build alone does not accept an application.

    FULL and RCVP are delivery targets

    FULL names the full specification target. RCVP names the required donor-complete preview target. These labels describe required scope, never an implementation-complete or accepted status.

    Coverage remains partial

    The exhaustive V2 feature inventory is unfinished. Counts are coarse milestone estimates. Unknown means unassessed; missing acceptance evidence remains unverified. Open a milestone to inspect its evidence and limits.
