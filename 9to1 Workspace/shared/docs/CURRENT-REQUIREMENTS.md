# Product and engineering requirements for shared code

Use the 22 November source register for product behaviour, app identities, interaction records and accepted visual decisions. Use the root contributor instructions for source custody and the native development loop. Earlier feature inventories and checks remain tied to their recorded revisions.

For each change, locate its owning app and typed action or service. Shared presentation belongs in CUI. Keep embedded and standalone hosts on the same object, permission decision and persistence path. Use platform adapters for operating-system concerns and donor engines behind their existing interface.

Review the expected result at normal and compact sizes, with keyboard navigation and the applicable accessibility and theme settings. Verify pending, denied and failed actions through the actual owner. Record the tested revision, scope and limits of the evidence; unresolved proposals remain outside approved product behaviour.
